191: ## Health and monitoring
192: 
193: `GET /liveness` responds without authentication and performs no signing. It is a
194: process liveness check, not a balance, Redis, or Solana RPC readiness guarantee.
195: Render uses it for `healthCheckPath`; later warmup pings may use the same path.
196: 
197: `GET /metrics` is also unauthenticated in this upstream version. On the shared
198: public listener, telemetry and signer public addresses are publicly visible.
199: Do not assume the API/HMAC middleware protects this endpoint. Changing exposure
200: requires a proxy or separate private monitoring listener and a reviewed design.
201: 
202: The actual balance gauge is `signer_balance_lamports`, labelled by `signer_name`
203: and `signer_pubkey`. The earlier `fee_payer_balance_lamports` name was a
204: documentation mismatch; the decision is to use the real gauge without an alias.
205: With this signer config, `signer_name` is `plotarmor-devnet` and `signer_pubkey`
206: must match the human-provided public address. Tracking runs every 30 seconds.
207: Upstream can report zero on an account-read error, so investigate RPC failures
208: alongside low-balance alerts. A no-signer process cannot supply balance samples.
